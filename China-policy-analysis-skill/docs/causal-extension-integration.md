# Causal extension — integration notes

The causal extension is integrated into `china-policy-risk-analysis`:

- [Political-economy causal engine](../references/political-economy-causal-engine.md): competing explanations, macro–micro causal identification, and adversarial checks.
- [Evaluation cases](../evals/cases.md): eight suggested cases and pass criteria for future behavioral evaluation.
- [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md): the `Causal Identification and Macro–Micro Coherence` section activates the reference; workflow step 3 tests political/security, fiscal/resource, principal-agent/implementation, and development/welfare constraints in parallel.

When hidden intentions are material, compare at least three distinct competing
explanations with differentiating predictions. The four categories are
candidates, not mandatory slots or a predetermined ranking. Retain
counterevidence and falsification conditions, and leave motives unidentified
when the evidence cannot distinguish them.

The existing pressure-valve and official-data references remain part of the
Skill. The causal engine supplements them with institutional choice sets,
macro-to-household transmission, data-vintage checks, and falsifiable scenarios.

For future revisions, distinguish static consistency and reference-path checks
from behavioral evaluation. The eight cases are evaluation specifications;
their presence does not establish that an evaluation was run or passed.
Record actual outputs and findings when evaluating them. Save dated predictions
and later observed outcomes separately for out-of-sample calibration.
