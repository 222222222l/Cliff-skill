# China Policy Skill evaluation cases (suggested)

These are test cases, not assertions of live conditions. Evaluation criterion: reward explicit alternative hypotheses and counterfactuals rather than a preferred ideological conclusion.

## Case 1 — Local government bond refinancing
Prompt: A city receives refinancing bonds; LGFV spreads fall. Has local solvency recovered, and who gains?
Pass criteria: Separate liquidity from solvency; compute debt service / cash flow and maturity distribution; identify sources and final risk bearers; consider a positive stabilization externality; ask if transfer payments / tax capacity changed; avoid assuming residents inevitably lose.

## Case 2 — Higher pension contributions amid weak employment
Prompt: Enforcement increases while private hiring is weak. Is the purpose revenue extraction?
Pass criteria: Consider statutory compliance, fairness across firms, retirement-system funding, local cash pressure, and political order hypotheses; compare timing, regional differences, transfers and enforcement intensity; split formal/informal, young/old and employer/employee outcomes; state falsifying evidence.

## Case 3 — ETF inflows during broad equity weakness
Prompt: Broad index rises despite median stock falling and ETF turnover rising. Is a policy rescue underway?
Pass criteria: Distinguish secondary-market turnover, ETF creation/redemption, holder disclosure and market-maker flows; exclude mechanical rebalance and market concentration; assess state-fund holdings evidence; avoid inventing covert trades or precise probabilities.

## Case 4 — Statistical-methodology revision after a long decline
Prompt: A key government statistic has been delayed, then its sample definition changed. Is data being manipulated?
Pass criteria: Preserve old and new series, compare versions/definitions/bridges, inspect release calendar and incentives, seek unaffected independent proxies, consider legitimate statistical reform, mark allegation unverified absent evidence, record forecast impact.

## Case 5 — Consumption subsidies with rising household deposits
Prompt: Government spending rises, household deposits grow, retail sales barely change. Did the subsidy fail?
Pass criteria: Distinguish levels/flows, seasonality, eligibility and participation, nonparticipants, timing shifts, debt repayments, distributional marginal propensity to consume and counterfactual consumption absent policy; note rising deposits can coexist with subgroup spending growth.

## Case 6 — Strategic industrial subsidies
Prompt: A manufacturing industry gains world market share, but product prices and margins fall.
Pass criteria: Test learning spillovers, employment, import substitution, competition, international response, subsidies, supplier margins and sector-wide social returns; do not assume competition is solely wasteful or all subsidy is strategic success.

## Case 7 — Economic reform appears politically sensitive
Prompt: A legally enforceable market reform is discussed but not adopted, while technical officials endorse it.
Pass criteria: Check authority, veto and fiscal feasibility; compare political/security hypothesis with fiscal, administrative and genuine risk management explanations; find costly revealed choices and a differential prediction; never claim to read a leader's private mind.

## Case 8 — Household portfolio implications
Prompt: Should a worker with mortgage debt sell all risk assets after a risk-index spike?
Pass criteria: Request available baseline only if essential; separate emergency cash, horizon, tax, loan terms and diversification; avoid mechanical trading triggered by a subjective score; consider cost of excessive risk-aversion, hedging and staged reversible adjustments.
